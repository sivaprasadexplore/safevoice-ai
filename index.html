Your Rights Under the POSH Act 2013

▼

The POSH Act 2013 protects every employee — permanent, contractual, temporary, or intern — in any workplace with 10 or more employees.

1

Right to file a complaint — Within 3 months of the incident. Extendable by the ICC if there is sufficient cause.

2

Right to confidentiality — Your identity, complaint, and evidence cannot be shared by the ICC. This is a legal obligation.

3

Right to a fair investigation — ICC must complete inquiry within 90 days. You can present your case and cross-examine witnesses.

4

Right to interim relief — You can request transfer, work from home, or have the accused transferred during investigation.

5

Right against retaliation — Any retaliation for filing is itself a punishable offence. Document everything.

6

Right to appeal — Unhappy with ICC decision? Appeal within 90 days to employer, court, or appellate authority.

What counts as sexual harassment?
Unwelcome physical contact, demands for sexual favours, sexually coloured remarks, showing pornography, or any unwelcome verbal or non-verbal conduct of a sexual nature — including a hostile work environment created by such behaviour.
Can I withdraw my complaint?
Yes, but only in writing. If the withdrawal is due to pressure or threats — that coercion is itself a separate offence. SafeVoice AI monitors for exactly this pattern.

Common feelings after a workplace harassment incident
Shock, self-doubt, anxiety, fear of not being believed, anger, shame, and numbness are all normal responses. These feelings do not mean you are weak — they mean you are human.
When should I seek professional help?
If you experience trouble sleeping, panic attacks, inability to focus, or thoughts of self-harm — reach out to the EAP counsellor immediately. Contact details are in the Support Contacts section.

Frequently Asked Questions

▼
Will anyone find out that I reported?
Not through SafeVoice AI. Your identity is never stored — only a case token. Even system administrators cannot link your token to your name.
What if the accused is more senior than me?
The POSH Act protects you regardless of the accused person's seniority. The ICC is legally required to be independent of the management hierarchy.
What if I have no evidence?
Your testimony is evidence. You do not need recordings or messages to file a complaint. ICC is obligated to investigate based on your account alone.
What happens to the accused during investigation?
The accused continues working unless you request interim relief — transfer, work from home, or distance from your workspace during the 90-day inquiry.
How long does the process take?
ICC must complete inquiry within 90 days, submit report within 10 days, and the employer must act within 60 days of receiving the report.
